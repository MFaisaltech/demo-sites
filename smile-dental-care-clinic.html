About the clinic

Gentle, complete dental care close to home

Smile Dental Care Clinic is a dental clinic in Islamabad providing everyday dental care for patients of all ages. Whether you are coming in for a routine check-up, a toothache that can't wait, or a treatment to improve your smile, the clinic covers your dental needs in one visit-friendly location.

You will find us on the first floor of Shahnawaz Plaza on Sachal Sarmast Road — easy to reach, with evening hours six days a week so appointments fit around work and school.
