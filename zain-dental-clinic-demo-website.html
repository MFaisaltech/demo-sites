What we do

Treatments for every smile

Whether it is a nagging toothache, a long-overdue cleaning, or a full smile makeover, the clinic offers complete dental care for adults and children. Call for consultation rates on any treatment.

General Dentistry

Everyday dental care that keeps small problems from becoming painful ones — thorough check-ups, fillings for cavities, treatment for toothache and sensitivity, and honest advice on what your teeth need and what they don't.

  • Check-ups & examinations
  • Tooth fillings
  • Toothache & sensitivity care
  • Extractions
Call for consultation rates

Cleaning & Teeth Whitening

Professional scaling and polishing removes the plaque and tartar that brushing at home can't reach, leaving your teeth smooth and your gums healthy. Whitening treatments then lift everyday stains from tea, coffee and tobacco for a visibly brighter smile.

  • Scaling & polishing
  • Stain removal
  • Teeth whitening
  • Gum care
Call for consultation rates

Braces & Orthodontics

Crooked, crowded or gapped teeth can be straightened gradually and comfortably with braces. Orthodontic treatment improves not just how your smile looks, but how your teeth bite, clean and last — for teenagers and adults alike.

  • Metal braces
  • Teen & adult orthodontics
  • Smile alignment
  • Retainers & follow-up care
Call for consultation rates

Dental Implants

A missing tooth doesn't have to stay missing. Dental implants replace the root as well as the crown, so the new tooth looks, feels and works like your own — letting you eat, speak and smile with confidence again.

  • Single-tooth implants
  • Implant crowns
  • Replacing missing teeth
  • Bridges & dentures advice
Call for consultation rates

About the clinic

Dental care that puts you at ease

Zain Dental Clinic serves families across G-9 and nearby sectors of Islamabad from its location on Rohtas Road in G-9 Markaz — a short walk from the Karachi Company bus stop, right by D Watson.

  • Care for all ages — children, adults and seniors are all welcome.
  • Clean, sterilised instruments for every single patient, every time.
  • Clear guidance before treatment — options and consultation rates explained upfront when you call.
